Linc privacy policy

Last updated 11 November 2026. Applies to the Linc phone app (Google Play: com.delowe.linc, and the app.linc.android APK) and the Linc Windows app, made by deloweProjects.

The short version

Linc does not collect, store or share your data with us or anyone else. We run no servers that receive your information, there are no accounts, no analytics, no tracking and no ads. What Linc shows you moves only between your own phone and your own PC.

What Linc handles, and where it goes

To do its job, the phone app can access the following, only after you grant the matching Android permission:

  • Notifications, messages (SMS) and call information: shown on your PC, and replies you type there are sent from your phone.
  • Clipboard: text you copy is sent to your other device so you can paste it there.
  • Files, photos and the app list: so you can browse, send and copy files and open apps from your PC.
  • Screen and microphone: only when you start mirroring, or turn on the phone as the PC's microphone.
  • Network and Bluetooth information: to find your PC and keep the connection up.

This data goes directly between your two devices, over your own Wi-Fi, hotspot, Bluetooth or USB cable, and is encrypted (TLS). It does not pass through any server of ours. Linc keeps small local settings and logs on your own devices only, and you can delete them by clearing the app's data or uninstalling.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell, share or transfer your data to anyone. Nobody at deloweProjects can see it.

Children

Linc is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect anything from anyone.

Your control

You can revoke any permission in Android's settings, unpair a PC in Linc, or uninstall Linc at any time. Because we hold none of your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions: message the developer on Telegram at t.me/LincApp, or open an issue at github.com/deloweProjects/LincApp.
