Get started

Three steps. About two minutes.

Install on your PC

Download Linc-Setup and run it. No admin prompt. If Windows says it protected your PC, choose More info, then Run anyway.

Get it on your phone

Android is in closed testing. Sign up with your Google Play email, then install Linc from Google Play once you are added.

Add a phone

On the PC choose Add a phone and point the phone's camera at the code. Done. It reconnects by itself from now on.

Private by design

There is no server to trust.

Most phone-to-PC apps route your screen, messages and files through a company's cloud. Linc doesn't have one.

  • Direct and encrypted. Your phone and PC talk to each other, and nobody else is in the conversation.
  • No account. Nothing to sign up for, nothing to log in to.
  • No telemetry. No backend means nothing to shut down the day interest fades.
  • Verifiable. Every release ships SHA-256 checksums, and updates are hash-checked before they install.

Questions

Good to know

Is it really free?

Yes. No ads, no subscription, no account.

What do I need?

A Windows PC (Windows 10 version 1809 or newer) and an Android phone (Android 11 or newer). They connect over the same Wi-Fi, a USB cable, or a hotspot.

Windows warned me when I ran the installer.

Linc is a small independent project and the installer isn't code-signed yet, so SmartScreen warns the first time. Choose More info, confirm the file name says Linc-Setup, then Run anyway. You can verify it first against CHECKSUMS.txt on the release.

How do I get the Android app?

Linc for Android is in a closed test on Google Play. Fill in the sign-up form with the email address you use on Google Play. Once you are added, the Google Play page lets you install it.

Why do I have to sign up first?

Google requires new Android apps to run a closed test with real testers before they can go public. Signing up just adds your Google Play account to that test. There is no cost and no account with us.

Does my data go through anyone's server?

No. The link is direct between your two devices and encrypted. See our privacy policy.

Where do I get help or news?

On Telegram at t.me/LincApp, or open an issue on GitHub.
